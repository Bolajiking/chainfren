## Company ambition

We want Africans to distribute, own, and earn on open rails built by Africans. Reaching that ambition will take useful products, open standards, and work from many companies and communities.

Chainfren aims to become foundational infrastructure for that ownership economy and a contributor to the wider open ecosystem. We will earn that role through public work that gives customers more control. We will also keep present work separate from future direction.
